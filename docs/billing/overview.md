# Billing & Pricing

Hostly offers transparent, usage-based pricing with no surprise bills.

---

## Pricing Principles
- **Billed Per-Second**: You only pay for the exact vCPU-seconds and RAM-seconds your services consume.
- **Zero Egress Markups**: We do not penalize you for bandwidth. Normal internet traffic is included.
- **Hard Budget Caps**: Set a maximum monthly spend (e.g. $25/mo or KES 3,500/mo). If your workloads reach this limit, Hostly protects you from unexpected credit card charges.
- **Multi-Currency Support**: Pay natively in USD ($) or KES (via M-Pesa).
